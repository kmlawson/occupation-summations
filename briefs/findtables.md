# Brief: find the pages that hold statistical tables

You are scanning contact sheets of a U.S. Occupation monthly report (Japan, GHQ/SCAP; or Korea, USAMGIK; 1947). Each contact sheet shows 8 page images in a 4 x 2 grid. The caption under each page is its file name, `p-NNN.jpg`, where NNN is the PDF page number.

Look at each contact sheet with the Read tool, and list every page that holds a **statistical table**: a grid of figures or entries in rows and columns, printed as a table. Tables stand out at thumbnail size as ruled or aligned columns of numbers.

Do NOT list:
- the report's table of contents, lists of charts, or section contents lists ("Paragraph 1 ... 2 ...");
- pages of running text, charts and graphs without printed figures, maps, cover pages, or blank pages.

If you cannot tell from the thumbnail, open the full page image (same folder as given in your task, same file name) with the Read tool and decide. Do not run OCR, scripts or any image-description service; look at the images yourself.

Return the page numbers (integers) of every page with at least one table.
