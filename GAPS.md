# Gaps in the collection

Recorded 6 October 2026 for later follow-up. Issue numbers and page numbers are from this collection: 71 PDFs, plus 8 issues found bound inside other PDFs. Page numbers are PDF pages.

Korea Nos. 1–5 are not separate reports. They are the Korea parts of the joint *Summation of Non-Military Activities in Japan and Korea*, held here as J-01, J-03, J-04 and J-05. So Korea's only gap among Nos. 1–5 is No. 2, the same as Japan's.

## 3. Incomplete or damaged copies

- **K-32, Korea No. 32 (May 1948):** PDF pp. 47–113 and 134–137 are pages from the July 1947 report (No. 22), misbound in place of May 1948 sections. They carry a different declassification stamp (NND 785084 rather than 785083). The May 1948 sections they replaced are Industrial Production, Mining, Allocations, Imports and Exports, Labor, Transportation, Communications, and parts of Finance. **This gap is filled** by the complete bound copy of No. 32 in K-30 (pp. 450–655).
- **K-22 (No. 22, July 1947):** check whether the misbound July 1947 pages in K-32 duplicate pages in K-22, or supply pages that K-22 lacks.
- **J-04-nlm** (NLM copy of No. 4): several pages produced garbage OCR in Chinese or Japanese characters (pp. 4, 6, 70, 74, 80, 90, 112, 120, 122, 208). The scan is probably fine and the OCR failed; re-OCR those pages or check them against J-04.
- **Single pages with garbage OCR:** J-08 p. 2, K-26 p. 269, K-27 p. 135.
- **K-31 p. 29:** the OCR gives only "###" placeholders.
- **J-35:** several section-opening pages are image-only (pp. 33, 37, 51, 80, 123, 132, 154), so their headings are missing from the text.
- **Charts and maps:** throughout, these are images with no OCR text. Their captions survive, but the data in them is not searchable.

## 4. Things to check

- **J-28** (Japan No. 28, January 1948) has 367 pages, about twice the length of its neighbours (160–200). Check whether it holds a second copy, an appendix or double-page scans.
- **Near-blank pages:** K-10, K-12 and K-21 each have about 22–26 near-blank pages among 85–117. They are probably blank versos, but confirm that no text pages are missing.
- **Printed page numbering:** the Japan reports' printed page numbers run well past their PDF page counts (No. 16 prints up to p. 255 in 137 PDF pages). This looks like blank or one-sided pages that were not scanned, but compare the printed table of contents against the scan for any report whose sections look short.
- **Internet Archive:** Japan No. 6 (`summation-non-military-activities-japan-no-06-1946-03`) was still waiting for IA to process it on 5–6 October 2026, and still lacks the `non-military-summation` subject.
- **Internet Archive metadata:** the IA titles of K-24, K-26, K-28, K-30 and K-33 name only the first issue in each volume. Consider adding the buried issues to their titles or descriptions.
